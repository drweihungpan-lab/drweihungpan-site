# drweihungpan-site

The language of this site: who it is for, the kinds of work it describes, and
the distinctions that keep them honest. Terms here are the canonical ones for
content, page copy, and code.

## Audience

**Researcher**:
A person who owns a study and needs help with its data. A physician, an
academic, or a graduate student — the segment is defined by the need, not by
career stage or seniority.
_Avoid_: Student, physician, academic (as names for the segment), user

## Kinds of work

These name what he does, not things a visitor can buy. The site describes them;
it does not offer, price or scope them.

**Engineering**:
Building the data infrastructure a study needs: pipelines, reproducible
analysis setups, apps and dashboards, registry databases. Named alongside
Analysis — the combination is the differentiator, not either half.
_Avoid_: Dev work, IT, software support, technical services

**Analysis**:
Study design, running the statistics for a Researcher, and delivering results,
figures and reproducible code.
_Avoid_: Statistical support, consulting (reserve "consulting" for the biotech
and academic-group work)

**Coaching**:
Teaching one Researcher to run their own analysis. The Researcher does the work
and leaves able to defend their own methods.
_Avoid_: Tutoring, training, help

**Workshop**:
Paid group training delivered to a team or organisation, derived from a Course.
_Avoid_: Seminar, session, training (unqualified)

**Course**:
The public teaching material a Workshop is delivered from, held in its own
public repository. Free to read; the Workshop is the delivery of it.
_Avoid_: Curriculum, syllabus (a syllabus is part of a Course, not the Course)

## Distinctions

**Separation**:
The rule that the professional record and the personal life never share a page.
A Researcher evaluating him meets no faith content and never reads his current
employer's name beside a description of work he takes on; a visitor who reads
about his faith chose to open that door. It governs where something may appear,
not what is worth saying.
_Avoid_: Firewall, boundary, segregation, church and state
